The first downloadable version of Inbox Tidy, a small Mac app that shows who fills your inbox, unsubscribes you, and moves old email to Trash.

**Download the file for your Mac.** Not sure which one? Apple menu › About This Mac.
- **Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4…): `Inbox-Tidy-0.2.0-macOS-Apple-Silicon.zip`
- **Intel**: `Inbox-Tidy-0.2.0-macOS-Intel.zip`

Unzip it, drag Inbox Tidy into Applications, and open it. The first time, macOS asks you to confirm, because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate yet. The [README](https://github.com/siddnikh/inbox-tidy#download-easiest) walks you through it.

**What's in it**
- Connects to Gmail, iCloud Mail, Yahoo Mail, Rediffmail Pro, and other IMAP providers with app passwords, which are kept in your Mac's Keychain.
- Shows who emails you most, how often, and how many you open, from email headers only, over the last 12 months.
- An AI helper sorts your senders and suggests who to drop. It can be Claude Code, Codex, or an OpenRouter key and model that you choose in Settings, and Settings walks you through setting it up.
- Unsubscribes for you, automatically or by email. Senders that only offer a web page open in your browser, and you tick them off on Home once you've finished.
- Moves a sender's old emails to your provider's Trash, where they can be recovered.
- Home shows what you've achieved, one email account at a time or all together, and a live card while a scan runs.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. Check a download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
